# Changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-23

- Initial public release.
- Bundles 92 first-pass manually reviewed Chinese cinematic prompt examples as structured text and fields.
- Adds one-shot contract, motion-budget guidance, conflict checks and model-adapter boundaries.
- Adds 12 evaluation prompts covering preservation, image-to-video, multi-shot detection, sample citation, unknown models and lighting conflicts.
- Original stills/GIFs are intentionally excluded from the public package; source video/time evidence remains in the text library.
